niece and is published with her permission. That drawing and everything
derived from it — the animation frames in `cuadros/` (Wawi 1's `wawi/`),

not under the MIT license that covers the code.

You may copy, adapt and share them for non-commercial purposes, with credit
("Wawi, drawing by Marcos Senatori's niece") and an indication of any changes.
For commercial use, replace the art with your own: the code runs without it.
Make your own pet pack (see `docs/MASCOTAS.md`) and choose it in the menu;
without this folder the mascot simply does not appear and only the menu bar
item is left.

Changes in Wawi 2: the frames were moved from `frames/` to `cuadros/` and are
described by `mascota.toml`, the pet pack manifest. The pictures themselves
are unchanged.
